Course Modules

👥 Who This Is For

Data analysts, BI professionals, reporting specialists, project managers, operations teams, and business users who already understand Power BI basics and want to move beyond simple charts into complete analytics solutions.

✅ Prerequisites

Basic familiarity with Power BI Desktop. Comfortable with Excel-based reporting, common data concepts (tables, relationships), and creating simple charts. No DAX or Power Query experience required.

🏁 Course Outcome

Connect and transform business data, design an optimised semantic model, write practical DAX calculations, build interactive dashboards, publish to Power BI Service, configure refresh and security, and deliver a complete end-to-end analytics solution.

📂 Case Study: RR EngineerWorks

A fictional mid-size manufacturing and regional sales company. Every module builds on the same dataset: sales orders, production output, regional targets, product catalogue, customer master, and workforce data.
